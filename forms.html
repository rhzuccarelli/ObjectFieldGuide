Forms

Edges, corners and volumes. Chosen for the hand and for light.

01How to read this volume

Form is what an object looks like before anyone reads a label. Each entry here is a species of edge, corner or volume: how to recognise it, when it is right, how it catches light, and how it behaves when you actually make it. Forms are chosen for the hand and for light first, and for the screen last.

Numbers marked [verify] are starting points, not laws. Light is the only reviewer that never lies about a radius.

02Three questions
02.1Where does the light go? An edge is seen as the line of light it makes. A radius makes a soft band, a chamfer a sharp line, a sharp edge almost nothing. 02.2Where does the hand go? Edges that are held need a radius. Edges that are only seen can be as sharp as the material allows. 02.3How is it made? Every process has edges it likes: FFF prints chamfers better than fillets on the bed, moulds need draft, metal can hold a sharp edge that plastic cannot.
