Object Field Guide

How objects are joined, touched, proportioned and shaped. With reasons.

01About

A field guide identifies species in the wild. This one does the same with the parts of objects: the joints that hold them together, the buttons and knobs that people touch, the proportions and forms that decide how they look. Each part is a species, with the marks to identify it, when to use it and when not, how to proportion and make it, its look-alikes, and its habitat: real products where it lives.

It is the working guide of an industrial designer, written down so the decisions do not have to be made twice. It is also for students, who will make them for the first time. Both are welcome to disagree.

A companion to the Sound Field Guide.
